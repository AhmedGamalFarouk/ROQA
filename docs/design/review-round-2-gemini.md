## What works well
- **Seamless Clock Morphing:** The transition from the 15 DIP bold pill clock to the 30 DIP panel header completely eliminates the crossfade blink identified in Round 1. The clock element remains continuously visible and solid throughout every single frame in [`13-roqa-morph-frames.png`](file:///C:/Users/Ahmed/.t3/projects/roqa/artifacts/design-review-2/evidence/13-roqa-morph-frames.png).
- **Dynamic Region Fitting & Hover Intent:** Dynamic per-frame region updates (`layout::notch_region`) with a two-frame lead ensure crisp visual boundaries without pixel clipping while keeping the screen edge clickable. Sideways pointer passes within 320 ms reliably cancel without false expansions.
- **Dock Polish:** The lifted $48 \times 48$ hover chip, subtle $10\%$ white inner hairline, 3 DIP icon lift, and the newly verified $12 \times 3$ active window indicator pill ([`12-roqa-dock-active.png`](file:///C:/Users/Ahmed/.t3/projects/roqa/artifacts/design-review-2/evidence/12-roqa-dock-active.png)) establish high visual consistency with Nusa's material palette.
- **Robust Host Compatibility:** Dynamic resolution of `DCompositionWaitForCompositorClock` preserves teardown and load safety across older Windows builds while maintaining compositor clock pacing on Windows 11.
